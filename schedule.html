Collection Guidelines

  • ✓ Place bins at least 3 feet apart from each other
  • ✓ Keep bins 5 feet away from cars, mailboxes, and other obstacles
  • ✓ Close bin lids completely to prevent spillage
  • ✓ Don't overload bins - material should not extend above the rim

Missed Collection?

If your bin wasn't collected on the scheduled day:

  1. Keep your bin out - it may be collected later that day
  2. Check if there's a holiday delay (collections shift by 1 day)
  3. Contact us at (+211) 911-111-111 if not collected within 24 hours
  4. Report the issue through our website for faster resolution
