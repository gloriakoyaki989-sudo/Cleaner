Recycling Best Practices

  • •Rinse all containers before recycling
  • •Remove lids and caps from bottles
  • •Flatten cardboard boxes to save space
  • •Keep recyclables dry and clean
  • •Don't bag recyclables - keep them loose

Common Mistakes

  • ✗Pizza boxes with grease stains
  • ✗Plastic bags (return to stores instead)
  • ✗Shredded paper (use compost bin)
  • ✗Contaminated containers with food residue
  • ✗Wire hangers (donate or special recycling)
